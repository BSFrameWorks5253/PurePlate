Milk Starch Test

Target: Added Starch / Detergent
🧪 You will need:
  • 1 small transparent glass cup
  • Tincture of Iodine solution (2-3 drops)
  • 3-5 ml milk sample (boiled & cooled)
Science Principle

Iodine reacts with amylose starch molecules to form a characteristic deep blue/purple charge-transfer complex. Pure milk remains creamy white or pale yellow.

⚠️ Safe at-home protocol. For students, perform under adult supervision.
